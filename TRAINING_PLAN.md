# Training Plan

_Built from your Strava data, June 2026. Zone 2 base-building after a 5-month grey-zone plateau._

## ⚠️ Right now: tibialis (shin) tightness
Front-of-shin muscle tight after cold max broad-jumps; lifting the foot up is restricted.
- **No running, no jumping/plyometrics** until it moves freely and is pain-free.
- Gentle **pain-free** ankle movement, light massage, easing the tightness. Stretch the
  calf and the front of the shin gently.
- **See a physio/doctor if:** still tight/worse after 1–2 weeks, pain *on the bone*,
  swelling, pain at rest/night, or you can't bear weight comfortably.
- Do NOT "test" it with effort. Testing it is what caused this.

## Pace zones (estimated — no HR recorded, so these are a map not gospel)
| Zone | Use | Pace (min/km) | Talk test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 2 | Easy — 80% of running | **7:45 – 8:00** | Full sentences |
| Zone 3 ⚠️ | AVOID (the trap) | 6:55 – 7:30 | A few words |
| Zone 4 | Threshold / 1 hard run | **6:30 – 6:50** | Comfortably hard |
| Zone 5 | Intervals, short reps | **6:00 – 6:20** | Can't talk |

Rule when no watch zones: **talk test wins.** Can't speak a full sentence on an easy run? Slow down.
Refine these by recording HR or running a 5K time trial when healed.

## Golden rules
1. **Finish easy.** Last km same pace as the first. No sprint finishes — they wreck zone 2.
2. **80% of running is zone 2.** Easy means easy.
3. One hard run per week, max — and only in Phase 3.

## Phase 0 — Recover (now → foot pain-free)
No running/jumping. When walking is pain-free for 20+ min, add:
- **Tibialis raises:** stand, lift toes toward shin — 2×15.
- **Calf raises:** 2×15.
These rehab the exact area and build the base for safe explosiveness later.

## Phase 1 — Base, 3 runs/week, all Zone 2 (first ~8 weeks back)
- **Run 1 — Easy:** 30 min @ ~7:50. Finish easy.
- **Run 2 — Easy + strides:** 30 min easy, then 4×15-sec relaxed strides (~80%, NOT max),
  full walk recovery. This is where the "go fast" urge goes — safely.
- **Run 3 — Long easy:** 40–50 min, zone 2, conversational throughout.

## Phase 2 — Add explosiveness (only after ~3–4 weeks pain-free in Phase 1)
Low and progressive, 2×/week, a few minutes only, always after warming up:
- Start: 2-foot pogo hops + skipping, 2×10.
- Build over weeks toward bounds, then broad jumps.
- **Never test max output cold again.** Power is built in small high-quality doses.

## Phase 3 — One quality run/week (after 8+ weeks of base, pain-free)
One of the three runs becomes real work: hills or a tempo block in Zone 4 (~6:40).
The other two stay easy forever.

## The 8-week test
Does **7:50/km feel easier**, or can you hold it longer than before?
That's the aerobic engine the hard months never built.
