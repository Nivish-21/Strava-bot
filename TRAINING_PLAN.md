## ⚠️ Read first — current shin strain
Front-of-shin muscle (tibialis anterior) tight after cold max broad-jumps; lifting the
foot up is restricted. Until it moves freely and is pain-free:
- **No running. No jumping/plyometrics (broad jumps OUT).** No sprint finishes.
- Upper-body Push & Skill work can continue (doesn't load the shin) — pain-free only.
- Your own tibialis raises / soleus holds / foot intrinsics become **active rehab** — do
  them gently now.
- **See a physio if:** still tight/worse after 1–2 weeks, pain *on the bone*, swelling,
  or pain at rest/night.

## DAY 1 – PUSH
- Explosive Push-Up — 3×5. 🔸 **While injured / first weeks back: fast push-ups with max
  intent instead** (no jump). Return to explosive once healed.
- Archer Push-Up — 3×4/side (left leads)
- Pike Push-Up, feet elevated, 3-sec eccentric — 3×5
- Diamond Push-Up — 3×6
- Pseudo Planche Lean Hold — 3×15 sec
- Fingertip OR Towel Push-Up — 3×8 (left-hand focus)

## DAY 2 – RUN + CORE + NECK
**Run — all phases: Easy Zone 2, 7:45–8:00, finish easy.**
- Phase 1: 20–25 min (build to 30). Rain: skip run, +10 min core as slow controlled work.
- Core/Neck: Dead Bug 3×6/side · Copenhagen Plank 3×20 sec · Neck Flexion+Extension (hand resistance) 3×12 each.

## DAY 3 – PULL (FLOOR) + LEGS + KNEES
- Superman Hold + Scapular Squeeze — 3×10 (2-sec hold)
- Reverse Snow Angel (prone) — 3×12
- Australian Pull-Up (table) OR bedsheet row — 3×8
- 🔸 **Broad Jump — REMOVED until shin pain-free.** Reintroduce in Phase 2 progressively
  (pogo hops → bounds → broad jumps). Never cold.
- Bulgarian Split Squat (left leads) — 3×6/side
- Single-Leg RDL (bodyweight, left leads) — 3×6/side

**Knee block — every Day 3 and Day 5, no skipping:**
- Spanish Squat OR Wall Sit, toes elevated — 3×30 sec
- Terminal Knee Extension (band) OR Slow Straight-Leg Raise — 3×15

## DAY 4 – RUN + CORE + NECK
**Run — phase-dependent:**
- **Phase 1 (base, ~8 weeks): Easy Zone 2, 25–30 min, then 4×15-sec relaxed strides**
  (~80%, NOT max), full walk recovery. Strides safely feed your explosiveness urge.
  *No 1-min hard intervals yet — that repeats the plateau.*
- **Phase 3 (after base + healed):** the interval day returns — warmup walk → 4–5×1 min
  hard (Zone 5, 6:00–6:20) → 2 min walk → cooldown. Build interval count back up slowly.
- Core/Neck: Hanging Knee Raise 3×8 (or Dragon Flag Negative 3×5) · Back Extension/Superman 3×12 · Neck Lateral Flexion 3×10/side.

## DAY 5 – FULL BODY SKILL
- Wall Handstand Hold (chest to wall) — 3×20 sec
- Tuck Planche Lean OR Tuck L-Sit — 3×10–15 sec
- Pseudo Planche Push-Up — 3×4
- External Rotation (water bottle, left leads) — 3×12
- Wall Sit, toes elevated — 3×30 sec
- Foot Intrinsics — Towel Scrunch OR Short Foot — 3×20 _(do these now — direct shin/arch rehab)_

## DAY 6 – RUN + CORE + NECK
**Run — all phases: Long Easy Zone 2, conversational throughout.**
- Phase 1: 35–45 min easy. Rain: 30 min brisk walk + core only.
- Core/Neck: Plank Shoulder Tap 3×8/side · **Tibialis Raises 3×20** · **Soleus Hold 3×40 sec** · Chin Tuck with Resistance 3×12.
  _(Tibialis + soleus are your shin rehab — prioritise them now.)_

## DAY 7 – REST

## Phase map
- **Phase 0 (now):** shin rehab. Strength upper/skill OK pain-free; no running, no jumping.
- **Phase 1 (~8 weeks back):** 3 runs/week ALL easy zone 2 (Days 2, 4-with-strides, 6).
  No intervals, no broad jumps yet.
- **Phase 2 (after ~3–4 weeks pain-free):** reintroduce explosiveness progressively —
  pogo hops + skipping → bounds → broad jumps. Explosive push-ups return.
- **Phase 3 (after 8+ weeks base, fully healed):** Day 4 becomes the one quality/interval
  run. Everything else stays easy.

## The 8-week test
Does **7:50/km feel easier**, or can you hold it longer than before? That's the aerobic
engine the hard months never built.
